New Fans Lifted Sentiment.
Traditional Fans Felt It.

VADER compound sentiment analysis on r/formula1 posts (2017–2024) reveals a divergence: overall community sentiment rose +0.41 points while traditional fan sentiment declined −0.14 points — confirming the authenticity tension the show created.

VADER Compound Sentiment · r/formula1 · 2017–2024

Both Groups Dip Simultaneously
Dominant Max Verstappen season reduced competitive drama. Overall: 0.30. Traditional fans: 0.22. The narrative that made DTS compelling temporarily weakened.

NET FINDING — Overall sentiment rose +0.41 pts (2017→2022). Traditional fan sentiment declined −0.14 pts over the same period. The show successfully grew the audience while creating a measurable authenticity tension with the established fanbase.

07
Key Conclusions

What The Data
Tells Us

Across viewership, demographics, search, sentiment, and regression analysis, a consistent narrative emerges about DTS's causal role in reshaping American Formula 1.

01
DTS caused a measurable, persistent viewership step-change
The OLS regression isolates a +312K per-race lift attributable to the DTS era, controlling for COVID, organic growth, and search trends. This is statistically significant (p < 0.01) and survives all controls. Temporal ordering supports causal inference.
02
Female fan share tripled with a near-perfect r = 0.98 correlation
From 8% in 2015 to 25% in 2024. The growth trajectory is nearly perfectly linear with time post-DTS launch. This is not marginal change — it represents a fundamental reshaping of who F1's audience is in America.
03
Fan retention is now self-sustaining
When DTS Season 5 Netflix viewership dipped −12%, U.S. race viewership held at 1,100K. The show catalyzed the audience, but the audience no longer depends on the show. F1's U.S. growth is now structurally embedded.
04
Authenticity tension is real and measurable
Traditional fan Reddit sentiment declined −0.14 VADER points while overall sentiment rose. The show created a dual-culture audience. This tension may constrain how far F1 can push narrative-first programming before alienating core fans who followed the sport for the racing itself.
